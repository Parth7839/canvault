How it works

  1. Tap Scan, fit the can in the frame and snap. Keep snapping to scan several.
  2. Every can earns XP. Rarer cans, new lines and daily streaks earn more. Rank up and unlock achievements.
  3. Name, rarity and price fill in automatically. Prices are estimates in ₹, so check them.
  4. Tap any can to edit it, set a quantity, star it or move it to your wishlist.
  5. Check Stats to see what your vault is worth.
